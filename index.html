Frequently Asked Questions

How is WPM calculated? +

WPM is calculated by taking the number of correctly typed characters, dividing by 5 (standard word length), and normalizing it over a 60-second interval.

What is Zen Mode? +

Zen Mode hides the timer and stats to help you focus purely on the text without the pressure of a ticking clock, which often improves accuracy.

How do I recover my account? +

Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen. You will need to provide your username and the answers to the security questions you set during registration.

Can I use TypeMaster on mobile? +

While the site is responsive, it is optimized for physical keyboards to provide the most accurate speed measurement.

Is my data shared with anyone? +

No. All your typing data and records are stored locally in your browser's secure storage and are only accessible via your ID.

How can I reach 100+ WPM? +

Focus on accuracy first. Speed is a byproduct of muscle memory. Practice daily in our Arcade mode to improve finger reflex and key positioning.

Is there an offline mode? +

Since the app uses LocalStorage, you can actually access your records as long as you are using the same browser, even if your connection drops.
